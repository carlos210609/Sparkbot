# SparkBot M16–M150

This layer turns the milestone roadmap into executable local contracts.

## Reliability: M16–M30
Eventing, retry/backoff, circuit breaking, quotas, leases, idempotency, scheduling,
budgets and recovery-safe primitives.

## Security & privacy: M31–M45
Secret redaction, PII scrubbing, tamper-evident audit, prompt-injection signals,
webhook signatures, OAuth state replay protection and action boundaries.

## Research & knowledge: M46–M60
Typed sources, evidence packs, unsupported-claim detection, research planning,
consistency checks and provenance.

## Computer use & social: M61–M75
Browser action classification, manual security checkpoints, high-impact boundaries,
content drafting and platform-isolated drafts.

## Growth, CRM & commerce: M76–M100
Lead scoring, funnel conversion, experimentation, pricing bounds, revenue ledger,
campaign measurement and reporting seams.

## Engineering & DevOps: M101–M120
Content-addressed artifacts, health checks, feature flags, change/release gates,
rollback metadata and a kill switch.

## Evaluation & optimization: M121–M135
Metric gates, regressions, drift, canaries and guarded optimization.

## Platform & scale: M136–M150
Plugin/API contracts, tenant registry, governance, capability inventory,
production readiness and final security/platform gates.

### Contract vs. external integration

M16–M150 are executable local foundations. They intentionally do not fake external
systems. Third-party APIs, payment processors, social networks, cloud accounts,
distributed queues, PostgreSQL/pgvector and deployment infrastructure must be
connected through these contracts before their corresponding production capability
can be declared operational.

A milestone is therefore **contract-ready**, not automatically **production-complete**.
